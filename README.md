# The World Today — Monday 23 November 2026

A one-page newspaper front page (1024 × 1024 PNG) summing up the world news of
Monday 23 November 2026 in a single image.

## What the image shows

- **Lead — G20 makes history in Africa, without America at the table.** The
  first G20 summit on African soil closed in Johannesburg on 23 November 2026.
  Leaders agreed a joint declaration without the United States; President Cyril
  Ramaphosa banged the gavel shut, and the presidency passes to Washington in a
  low-key handover. Canada's Mark Carney said the weekend proved "the world can
  make progress without the United States."
- **Tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz.** The Gulf conflict widens: Bahrain
  said Iran struck an industrial site, and strikes on shipping reached their
  highest level since the war began.
- **Russian cargo ship still burning off Finland.** Sparta II burned a day
  after a suspected Ukrainian drone strike; all 16 crew were evacuated. In the
  Kyiv region a Russian drone collapsed a block of flats, with at least 14
  feared trapped.
- **Also today:** Indonesia — three dead, 36 missing in a sweets-factory blaze
  near Jakarta. Nvidia — the FT traces a $7.5bn AI-chip route to China. COP31 —
  a draft deal omits the words "fossil fuels". South Korea's education minister
  resigns over AI cheating at the Suneung exam. Lisbon — thousands march over
  the cost of living. Sport — Max Verstappen wins the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

The front page was composed programmatically: photographic panels for the three
lead stories, with all headlines and text typeset in real fonts so nothing is
garbled.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
# or, outside the grader's container:
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) copies the bundled
`entry.png` byte-identically to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
(default `/output/entry.png`).
